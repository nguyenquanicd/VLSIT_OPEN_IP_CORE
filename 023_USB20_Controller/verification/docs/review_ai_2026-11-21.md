## Over-claims and errors found

1. Functional coverage is stimulus intent, not observed behavior (AI-RV-050). `B` markers are printed by the interpreter when the ops line is reached (`tb_usb2_utmi_axim.sv` line 428); no monitor samples the DUT. The plan says monitors count bins. The 82 % figure should be labelled "scenario markers issued", and the verification report 5.0 and 5.2 should not be read as measured functional coverage.
2. R-243 "none relaxed a check to get a pass" is too strong (AI-RV-051). Changes recorded in bugs.md that loosen, remove or re-target an expectation:
   - r0053 (line 46): the harness assertion "write strobes all ones" was removed; justification (partial strobes on the last beat are legal) is plausible and the memory compare remains, but no replacement strobe check was added.
   - r0073 to r0075 (line 51): the expected-packet time-out of the harness was lengthened (512-byte packet); justified by packet length.
   - r0096 to r0107 (line 53): suspend expected within 875 us became "within 10 ms"; justified by the cited REQ-FUNC-018 reading, which I could not confirm independently; STALL now expected for bLinkState other than L1 (LPM ECN).
   - r0250 to r0260 (line 60) and report 19.3: SOF check at TSCALE=16 widened to 7031..8593 ns (about +-10 %, `host_enum_bulk_dma_ts16_s1.ops` line 38) to hide the +-6 % jitter of the open finding FND-009; only one TSCALE=1 host run keeps the tight window.
   - r0263 to r0266 (line 61): `timeout` accepted for a lost response, host re-request accepted; follows ERR-004 text.
   - r0316 to r0319 (line 70): poll mask changed to first-bit polling.
   - FND-008 closure (line 79): four assumptions added to the FIFO formal harness and the receive cover lowered from 8 to 2 rows; each triaged from solver traces, but they shrink the proved space.
   - DEC-042: seven findings (FND-001, 002, 003, 004, 006, 010, 013) closed by declaring the observed RTL behavior as the specified behavior. For FND-003 (a corrupted packet after an AXI error response stays committed and can be sent on the bus) this makes the spec follow the RTL.
