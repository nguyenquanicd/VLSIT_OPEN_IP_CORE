### 2.3 Environment without a VIP (R-262)

No qualified USB, UTMI+, ULPI, APB or AXI VIP exists in this repository, and no UVM simulator is installed. The environment is a project-local harness: thin BFMs for the supported roles, passive monitors, an independent Python protocol model, assertions and counters. It is not a qualified full-protocol VIP; unsupported items are the out-of-scope rows above.
